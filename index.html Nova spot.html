NOVA SPOTEarth Analog Screening Explorer

Explore and compare eight terrestrial environments as partial analogs for lunar and Martian field research.

Preliminary. All scores are qualitative desk-screening estimates. They are not measurements, validated similarity coefficients, or mission-site recommendations. No NASA satellite data is integrated in this prototype.

Screening framework

Preliminary Screening Index = (S1 + S2 + S3 + S4) / 20 × 100

S1 Terrain similarity · S2 Geological relevance · S3 Environmental harshness · S4 Field-testing value. Each is scored 1 to 5, so the index ranges from 20 to 100. A higher index means more favorable on these four qualitative dimensions, not a validated match to the Moon or Mars.

Scientific limitations and future development

Current limitations

  • Scores are preliminary qualitative judgments, not measured values.
  • NASA satellite-derived measurements have not been integrated.
  • Earth cannot reproduce lunar vacuum, low gravity and radiation, or the full Martian environment. Every site is a partial analog.
  • Relevance labels and the established/candidate status are preliminary and need checking against analog-site literature.

Next development stages

  1. Integrate documented NASA Earth-observation datasets.
  2. Add relevant lunar and Martian reference datasets.
  3. Define measurable indicators.
  4. Normalize data with reproducible methods.
  5. Review analog-site literature to assess candidate novelty.
